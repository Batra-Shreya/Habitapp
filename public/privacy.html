🌱 Habitat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information the Habitat app ("Habitat", "we", "the app") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. We keep it short because the app itself is small and collects very little.

Information we collect

  • Account details — the username and passcode you choose. Your passcode is stored only as a salted, hashed value; we never store or see it in plain text.
  • Your habit data — the goals you create, when you mark them complete, and the points, streaks, and badges you earn.
  • Buddy connections — the username of a friend you choose to add as a buddy, so the app can show you each other's progress.
  • Basic technical data — standard server logs (such as request times) needed to run and secure the service.

We do not collect your real name, email, phone number, location, or contacts, and we do not use third-party advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: save your goals and progress, and sync them across your devices.
  • To power the buddy feature: show your chosen buddy a limited public snapshot of your progress (name, points, best streak, completion count, and online status) and deliver cheers between you.
  • To keep the service working and secure.

What your buddy can see

A buddy you add sees only your username, points, best streak, number of completions, and whether you're currently online. They cannot see your passcode, your login, or the names of your individual habits.

Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. Data is shared only as described above (with a buddy you choose), or if required by law.

Storage & security

Your data is stored on the server that runs the app. Connections are encrypted over HTTPS, and passcodes are hashed. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so please use a unique passcode and avoid putting sensitive information in habit names.

Keeping & deleting your data

  • You can remove a buddy at any time from the Buddy tab.
  • "Reset all data" in the app clears the data stored on your device.
  • To delete your account and its data from the server, contact us at the address below.

Children

Habitat is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above will change when we do.
